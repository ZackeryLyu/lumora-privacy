Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Lumora (“the app”) lets you try makeup shades on your face using your camera or your photos. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Lumora does not collect, store, send, or share your personal information.

Camera and photos

  • The app asks for access to the camera so it can show makeup on your face in real time.
  • The app can ask for access to your photo library if you choose to try shades on a photo you already have.
  • Camera images and photos are processed on your device only. They are not uploaded, saved by Lumora, or shared with anyone, including us.
  • The app finds the position of your face (such as eyes, lips, and cheeks) to place the makeup. This happens on your device, in memory, and is discarded when you leave the screen or close the app.

Microphone

The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

What we do not do

  • We do not have accounts or sign-in.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share any data, because we do not collect any.
  • The app works without an internet connection and does not send your data anywhere.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version at this address and update the date above.

隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 11 月 9 日

Lumora（“本应用”）让你用相机或已有照片试妆。本政策说明应用如何处理你的信息。

简要说明

Lumora 不收集、不存储、不发送、不分享你的个人信息。

相机和照片

  • 应用会请求相机权限，以便在你的脸上实时显示妆容。
  • 如果你选择用已有照片试妆，应用会请求相册权限。
  • 相机画面和照片只在你的设备上处理。不会上传，不会由 Lumora 保存，也不会分享给任何人，包括我们。
  • 应用会识别面部位置（例如眼睛、嘴唇和脸颊）来放置妆容。这一步在设备内存中完成，离开该画面或关闭应用后即丢弃。

麦克风

应用不录音，也不使用麦克风。

我们不会做的事

  • 没有账号，也不需要登录。
  • 没有广告，也没有数据分析。
  • 不会跨应用或网站跟踪你。
  • 不会出售或分享任何数据，因为我们不收集数据。
  • 没有网络也能使用，不会把你的数据发送到任何地方。

儿童

应用不会故意收集任何人的信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

变更

如果本政策有变更，我们会在这个地址发布新版本，并更新上面的日期。
